1. Who we are

  • Cloudly, Zernikepark 4, 9747 AN Groningen, the Netherlands
  • Chamber of Commerce (KvK): 95416137

  • Cloudly is the controller of your personal data as described in this policy.

2. What data we collect

  • Launch list: when you sign up, we store your email address, the form you used, the country you visit from (derived from your IP address, not the IP address itself) and the date and time.
  • Contact: when you email us, we keep your email and our reply.
  • Webshop (from launch): when you order, we process your name, address, email address, phone number if given, your order and your payment status. Your payment details go straight to the payment provider; we never see them.

3. Why we use this data

  • Launch list: to tell you when Cloudly is on sale and send you the early bird discount. We do this because you give us your consent.
  • Orders: to carry out, deliver and invoice your order or subscription. This is needed for our agreement with you.
  • Records: to meet the legal obligation to keep our accounts.

4. How long we keep data

  • Launch list: until you unsubscribe, and no longer than 12 months after launch if you have not become a customer by then.
  • Contact: as long as needed to handle your question, and no longer than 1 year after that.
  • Orders and invoices: 7 years, because the law requires this for our accounts.

5. Who we share data with

  • We never sell your data.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and stores the launch list. Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address to keep the site secure and fast.
  • From launch: our webshop, payment, shipping and email partners, only as far as needed to handle your order or send you emails.
  • Some partners may process data outside the European Union. This only happens with the safeguards the law requires, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. Cookies and browser storage

This website uses no tracking or advertising cookies and no analytics tools. The fonts are hosted on our own server, so your browser does not connect to other parties for them. We only use your browser's temporary storage (sessionStorage) to remember that you have seen the sign-up pop-up. This data stays on your own device and is cleared when you close the tab.

7. Your rights

  • You may access, correct or delete your data.
  • You may object to its use, restrict its use or receive your data in a file.
  • You can withdraw your consent for the launch list at any time. Email us or use the unsubscribe link in our emails.
  • Send your request to hello@drinkcloudly.com. We reply within 1 month.
  • If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

8. Security

We protect your data with appropriate measures, such as an encrypted connection (https) and access to the launch list only with a secret key.

9. Changes

We may update this privacy policy, for example when the webshop opens. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
