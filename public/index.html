FAQ

Good to know.

When can I buy Cloudly?

We are working on our first production run right now. Join the launch list and you will hear it first, with an early bird discount.

What is in a can of Cloudly?

Sparkling water, a little real fruit juice, natural caffeine from green tea, vitamins B3, B6 and B12, and natural sweeteners from stevia and erythritol. Vitamins B6 and B12 contribute to normal energy-yielding metabolism.

How much caffeine is in a can?

80 mg per 250 ml can, about one cup of coffee. High caffeine content: not recommended for children or pregnant or breast-feeding women.

Where will you deliver?

First in the Netherlands, with Belgium soon after.
